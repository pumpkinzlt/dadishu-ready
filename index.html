    Controls

    PC: Move the hammer with WASD / Arrow Keys, press Space to whack, P to pause, R to restart. You can also click/tap directly on moles.

    Mobile: Tap a mole to whack it, use the virtual D-pad for hammer movement, and tap the WHACK button for a quick hit.

    Item Usage

    Buy: Open Shop → Items and buy tools with Coins.

    Use in game: Tap the compact item dock. On PC, press 1-5 for Helmet, Bait, Combo, Stun, and Slow-Mo.

    Second Chance: This item is not shown in the dock because it triggers automatically when a run would end.
